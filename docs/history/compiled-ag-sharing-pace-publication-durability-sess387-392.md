## Standing lessons
- A wedge under AG sharing is usually a CONVOY through one shared AG; name the frozen item type and its owner's wait before touching the fence.
- Never close admission at ACQUIRE time for callers that may hold ILOCK/dirty trans; close at the UNLOCK (latch) and give unsafe callers a restart seam.
- Any new per-AG handoff state needs an explicit clear on EVERY completion path; the 30 s P275 watchdog is the symptom of a forgotten clear.
