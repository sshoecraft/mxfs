  What it took this session:

  1. The multi-session double-alloc blocker is root-caused and fixed. Instrumented reproduction (iter_14) captured the full braid: a cold disk read of an inode-cluster buffer that still had inode log items attached clobbered a
  just-iflushed dinode image (a dir block→shortform shrink); the queued writeback then pushed the stale pre-shrink image back to disk and the flush completion marked the inode clean, so the shrink existed only in RAM. Peers adopted the
  stale extent map, and the freed block's later legal reuse cross-linked two inodes. The fix — P150 READ-PRESERVE in pal/linux/xfs_buf.c — snapshots EX-held slots before such reads and restores them after the DMA (the read-side mirror of
  the existing partial inode write). Validated by four corruption-clean reproducer iterations at a pre-fix hit rate of ~1-in-2, then the full ladder.
  2. A self-inflicted perf regression caught and reverted: un-ratelimiting the P56 diagnostic print (0.10.118) alone dropped dlm_scaling@32 below its op-rate floor (~18K printk lines/4min/node); 0.10.120 restores the ratelimit.
  3. Testbed stall family eliminated: clyde was memory-exhausted (62GB qemu RSS + full swap), causing multi-minute I/O stalls, heartbeat purges, and MQTT barrier collapses. All 32 VMs were live-ballooned 4G→2.5G (persisted in config),
  and the swap debt was cleared.
