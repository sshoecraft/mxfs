## Recurring failure modes and operational rules

- **printk volume is a RULE-0 perf criterion**: un-ratelimiting P56 (~75 lines/s/node in unlink storms) ALONE dropped dlm_scaling@32 below its 50 ops/s floor. Per-op prints must be pr_warn_ratelimited or modarg-gated; a 20000 cap is not protection. Before criteria rows, check `journalctl -k | grep -c 'mxfs:'` in-window; >~30/s/node = probe taxing the run. Storm-time spam levers if at a floor edge: P25-INSTR, P61-ADOPT-CHK, P19-B3DEC, P82-ADD, P140-RECLAIM-COMMIT, P2L-*. ([[ccloop8ba7-sess7-printk-volume-is-a-perf-criterion]])
- **Host pressure masquerades as FS bugs**: 32 VMs @4G ballooned clyde to 62GB qemu RSS + 8G swap full → multi-minute I/O stalls → disklock heartbeat expiries/purges, 250s folio-lock waits, MQTT barrier collapses. Fix: `virsh setmem testN 2621440 --live --config` (all 32) + swapoff/swapon. Re-balloon after any virsh destroy/start if config didn't stick. MQTT broker = 192.168.1.149 (external, not clyde). ([[ccloop8ba7-sess7-clyde-memballoon-and-harness-timeouts]])
- **Never wrap run.sh in a timeout shorter than its per-node dispatch ceiling** (manifest budget, ×20 under RULE0_CALIBRATE; fio → 600s/node). Killing mid-fio O_DIRECT shutdowns 1–11 nodes ASYNC minutes later → pre-assert failures → forced re-prep (~4 prep cycles lost to this). Long tests: background uncapped, poll the log for 'done:'. After ANY killed run: `fuser -v /tmp/mxfs_run.lock`, kill the full timeout/sshpass chains, expect unmounted nodes, force fresh prep.
- **fio variance**: seqW aggregate 0.4–7.5GB/s run-to-run on the shared NVMe; fio_perf_vs_xfs worst(write)% dips <70% when the LUN is busy — rerun on a quiet LUN passes (seen at 4 and 32 nodes).
- **P-DBLALLOC content hits have false-positive families**: freed-block bytes persist on media (foreign-dead reuse), and after re-mkfs `disk_owner=<prev-FS ino>` is normal early. Only `disk_owner=<live THIS-FS ino>` or static xref (`scripts/xref_owners.py` on a QUIESCED image, all nodes unmounted) confirms a real double-alloc.
- **Journal pulls**: `journalctl -k --since 'T0' | gzip | base64 -w0` remotely, decode locally — NEVER grep the base64 stream (GNU grep binary-detects the 700KB line and eats it). test1-8 rotate FAST; pull per-iter immediately. Panic evidence lives in `/var/log/libvirt/qemu/testN-serial.log` (sudo -n tail) — kernel.panic=25 auto-reboots and the crashed boot's journald is GONE; VMs also netconsole to clyde:6666.
- **Prep flakes**: convergence stuck at active_count=N−1 ~1-in-2 → just retry with a new label; prep power-cycling slow-teardown nodes is normal self-healing.
- **Probe gating**: P136-DIRINO-WRDONE needs `mxfs_dirwr>=2 || instr`; repro default is only `dblalloc_probe=1` — a silent probe may be gated, not evidence. Join fallback: P-DIRDW `chg=` (di_changecount) is a monotonic TOTAL ORDER on dinode images — sort by chg, not wall time. ([[ccloop8ba7-sess6-note-dirdw-chg-total-order-join]])
- **Harnesses**: `scripts/dblalloc_repro.sh <label>` ~6 min/iter @32 (prep 46–150s, cc ~90s, sc ~16s, pm ~65s), auto-deploys current mxfs.ko, pulls journals, runs static overlap check; full probe set = `MXFS_EXTRA_MODARGS='dblalloc_probe=1 dirwr=1'`. `scripts/ladder_rung.sh <N>` runs a full rung serially uncapped → `tests/logs/ladder_rung_<N>caw.log`. run.sh filtered runs need marker srcversion match.
- **Method meta-lesson**: three consecutive wrong mechanisms (false-fresh discard → AG-meta time-travel → free-side) each rode a plausible unproven premise and each died to ONE targeted probe (P130/131=0, P143=0, P145 fired). The RULE 4 loop with a decisive discriminator per hypothesis is what converged in 7 sessions; the iter_10/12 detour came from inferring "never freed" out of a truncated probe window.
