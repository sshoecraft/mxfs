1. **FUA-platter read LAGS the SCST write cache.** mxfs_inode_disk_di_size FUA-reads the PLATTER; with fua_disable=1 (default) peers read the CACHE via plain reads. 3. di_size is a poor proxy under churn (concurrent ops move disk/incore independently).
