- Cutoff curve bucketed on OBSERVED peak (the run's `cutoff` file holds only the current value): no sweet spot at 260k (fable 1.15× worse at 175–300k, opus 0.95× wash n=6); cliff past ~300k; >420k 1.4–1.6× worse. Standing recommendation: 145k cutoff, effort high (high buys ~33% more loop hours than max from the same quota; the loop is credit-limited at ~100% duty), whichever model has credits. 500k cutoff REJECTED (selection confound: high-peak sessions are long sessions that had already amortised setup; asymmetric risk of degraded long-context recall on a codebase where a wrong edit corrupts a filesystem).
