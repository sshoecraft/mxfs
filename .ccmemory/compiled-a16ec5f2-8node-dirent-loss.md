## Root causes found & fixed (in discovery order)

### 1. FACE-1 — leaked i_lock EXCL (7+ sessions old) — FIXED `D0E47841`
[[sess1-FACE1-ROOT-FIX-torn-disk-skip-leaked-ilock]]: `mxfs_dlm_reload_inode`'s sess49b TORN-DISK-SKIP gate (xfs_mxfs_dlm.c ~12998) bailed on a divergent-grow holey disk map while still holding raw `down_write(&ip->i_lock)` and leaking `snap`. The reloader exits → dead write-holder → every later `mxfs_dir_drain_evict_data_blocks → down_read` blocks forever (dd D-state at drain_evict+0xf4). Tear observed cluster-wide → all 8 nodes leak the shared dir's i_lock same round → round-12 all-node create wedge, 0/8. Proof: `P-RELOAD-TORN-DISK-SKIP` fired on ALL 8 at the exact wedge instant. sess7's "P132 rm(dead)" was a misattribution. FIX: `kfree(snap); up_write(&ip->i_lock);` at the bail; drain_evict raw down_read → `mxfs_drain_ilock_read` (P132 forensics after 5s, bail only on FS shutdown). 4-node rarely tears → why FACE-1 was "8-node ~1/3 of runs".

### 3. Grant-epoch visibility ordering — FIXED `4FC99EB9` (first-ever 8/tcp PASS)

FIX: update/insert mirror (with dir_epoch) under table_rwlock FIRST, THEN signal; provisional-insert+unwind for unsolicited re-affirms. **This root recurs** — see #10/#11 below (other grant paths still deliver epoch=0).

## Recurring failure modes / lessons
- **Stale-base free-slot double-alloc RMW** is the unifying dirent-loss mechanism: whenever a modify runs with `dir_epoch/master_ep` UNESTABLISHED (grant path delivered epoch=0) OR over a concurrent-EX/torn/holey base, a peer's fuller image loses the whole-block writeback race and an entry is durably erased. Root #3 fixed the normal remote-grant path; #10 and #11 are the same class re-leaking through the EDEADLK-reacquire and local-upgrade grant paths — **audit ALL 4 grant/upgrade sites for dg_grant_ex**.
- **"Discard committed-but-unwritten state" class** recurs: SFTORN (#5, ILI fields), P91 iflush-strand (#8, STALE cluster buffer), CIL-window dir-data evict (#9 lead). Guard pattern = `mxfs_buf_has_uncheckpointed_mods` / never drop/adopt over pinned||ili_fields||in-AIL||in-CIL.
- Probe artifacts burned cycles: leaf magic@8-not-@0, P29 tag=CLOBBER, `[ ts]` padded bracket breaks awk $1, `nx=` greps matching `maxnx=`, dmesg spanning multiple runs (window by last `DRCph r=1 PHASE=create-start`; boot clocks skew ~10s → align by realns=). Verify `[ -e ]` cannot distinguish leaf-hash-present vs DSCAN-heal-masked.
